Why open innovation matters here

Family voices shouldn't live on someone else's server.

🔒 Private by design

Grandpa's voice, his stories and his health details never leave this laptop. No account, no upload, no terms of service.

🔁 Swappable brains

One environment variable swaps the model: Gemma, Llama, Qwen. Better Sinhala support tomorrow? Change one line.

💸 Free forever

No API bill for a hobby that should outlive any pricing page. Offline works in the kitchen, even with no signal.
